If you are starting an online business on a tight budget, the first question about systeme.io is not whether it's good. It's whether the systeme.io free plan is enough, or whether you'll hit a wall in two weeks and have to pay anyway.

This guide covers what the free plan actually includes according to systeme.io's own pricing page, where its limits usually start to bite, and who should upgrade. We won't promise results, and we only state numbers we could check against the official source.

Prices and limits verified on systeme.io/pricing on November 26, 2026. The page's FAQ says it was last updated in April 2026. Plans change, so confirm before you decide.

What the free plan includes (official numbers)

According to systeme.io's pricing page FAQ, the free plan supports:

  • 2,000 contacts
  • 3 sales funnels
  • 1 blog
  • 1 course
  • 1 affiliate program (so you can run your own affiliates)
  • 1 custom domain
  • Unlimited emails sent
  • 1 automation rule and 1 email campaign

The same FAQ says the free plan never expires and does not require a credit card. The pricing page also says payments through Stripe or PayPal carry 0% transaction fees from systeme.io on all plans. Your payment processor still charges its own fees.

One caveat: the pricing page isn't fully consistent. The "all plans include" section says unlimited students, while the FAQ mentions a 500-student limit on the free plan. If course enrollment matters to you, check the current terms before you build around either number.

Where the free plan is genuinely enough

For a lot of beginners, the free plan covers the whole first stage of a business:

Validating one offer. One funnel to collect emails, one to sell a product, and one spare is enough to test whether anyone wants what you're selling.

Building a starter email list. 2,000 contacts is a meaningful ceiling for a new creator. Since email sends are unlimited, you can send broadcasts to that list without paying per email.

Launching a single course. If your plan is one flagship course, the free plan lets you host and sell it.

Using your own domain. Many free tiers force a subdomain. Here you can connect one custom domain, which looks more professional from day one.

If that sounds like your next six months, you may not need to pay anything yet.

Where the limits start to bite

The free plan stops being enough at predictable points. These are the ones to plan for:

1. Automation

This is the limit most people underestimate. With 1 automation rule and 1 email campaign (a campaign is an automated email sequence), you can run a single welcome or nurture sequence. You can't run separate sequences for different lead magnets or products. If your strategy depends on segmenting people and sending them different follow-ups, you'll feel this one first.

2. Funnels

Three funnels go fast once you add a second product, a webinar registration page, or a separate lead magnet. Testing variations of the same offer also uses them up.

3. Contacts

When your list passes 2,000 contacts, you'll need the next plan. That's a good problem, but budget for it.

4. Courses

One course is fine for a single offer. A course library or a free mini-course plus a paid course means upgrading.

A note on sales caps

Some third-party reviews say the free plan has a lifetime sales limit. We couldn't find any mention of a sales cap on systeme.io's official pricing page or in the help center categories we checked. Because we couldn't confirm it either way, we're not stating a number. If you expect to sell seriously on the free plan, ask systeme.io support directly before you rely on it.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Free forever with no credit card, so trying it is genuinely risk-free
  • Email, funnels, courses, a blog and affiliate management in one place, which means fewer tools to connect
  • Unlimited email sends on every plan
  • No transaction fees from systeme.io
  • A custom domain on the free plan

Cons

  • Very tight automation limits on the free plan (1 rule, 1 campaign)
  • Only 3 funnels before you need to upgrade
  • Some inconsistencies on the pricing page (students), which make it harder to plan with confidence
  • An all-in-one tool means each feature is simpler than a dedicated specialist tool. Advanced email marketers or designers may find it basic.

Who the free plan is for

  • Beginners validating their first digital product, course or coaching offer
  • Creators who want to build an email list without paying per subscriber from day one
  • Side-hustlers who want one simple setup instead of five separate subscriptions
  • Anyone who wants to learn how sales funnels work before spending money

Who it's NOT for

  • Businesses that already have more than 2,000 subscribers. You'd start on a paid plan anyway.
  • Marketers who rely on complex, multi-branch automations from the start
  • Teams that need advanced design control or deep integrations with an existing tech stack
  • Anyone planning several products and funnels at launch. Budget for Startup from the beginning.

The bottom line

Is the systeme.io free plan enough? For one offer, one list and one course, yes. It's one of the more generous free tiers in the all-in-one category, and since it doesn't expire, you can build on it until you actually outgrow it. The real limits are automation (1 rule, 1 campaign) and funnels (3). If your plan depends on either, expect to move to the $17/month Startup plan sooner rather than later.

The sensible approach is to start free, build your first funnel, and upgrade when a specific limit actually blocks you, not before.
